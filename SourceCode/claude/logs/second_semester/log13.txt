> I believe there's another shortsighted issue in the CanShapeBePlaced() 
function of AutoEndDetection. Currently it seems only the center of the 
dummy shape is being checked for each position in the positionsToCheck and 
set at this line: dummyShape.position = centerPos. But shapes also consist 
of relativeTilePositions (in shapeData), only one of these positions in the 
shape need to be adjacent to another shape for it to be valid, at the moment
 only the center of the shape is being checked. When testing, this results 
in the game ending prematurely even if I still have a valid move, for 
example - the end position of a multi-position shape touching/adjacent to 
another placed shape is valid.
