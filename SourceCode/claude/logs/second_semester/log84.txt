● Here's what was wrong and what the updated fix does:

  The corner problem

  L-shape at center (0, 0) with rotation 2 (180°). Transformed tiles:
  - (0,0) — visible center
  - (-1,0) — out of bounds (x < 0)
  - (0,-1) — out of bounds (y < 0)
  - (0,-2) — out of bounds (y < -1)

  Three tiles off-grid in two different directions (left AND down). The
  previous fix only searched in the player's drag direction. Dragging right
  would only search rightward — but the shape also needs to move up to bring
   the below-grid tiles back. A pure rightward move, no matter how many
  steps, can never make all tiles valid because the negative-y tiles stay
  negative-y.

  Updated fix (ProcessTouchMovement)

  The movement search now has 4 priority levels:

  1. Exact drag direction — Try TryFindMovement(moveX, moveY) first. Works
  for normal drags, diagonal drags, and simple single-axis cases.
  2. Diagonal drag fallback — If the player dragged diagonally but the
  combined move is blocked, try each axis individually (preserving old
  sequential-axis behavior).
  3. Pure horizontal fails → try adding +1 Y then -1 Y (diagonal search to
  escape top/bottom corners)
  4. Pure vertical fails → try adding +1 X then -1 X (diagonal search to
  escape left/right corners)

  TryFindMovement searches up to 5 steps in the given direction (up from 3),
   finding the first valid position. Movement is applied directly to
  position/transform.position (bypassing the single-step Move* methods that
  would block on intermediate invalid positions).
