● The issue was in GetStarCountForShape() in ShapeManager.cs:588. Here's the
   root cause:

  How stars are attached to shapes (AddStarVisualToShape, line 482-503):
  - SingleShape: Star is a direct child of shape.transform
  - Multi-tile shapes (T, L, Square, Line, Z): Star is attached to a random 
  child tile via shape.transform.GetChild(randomChild), making it a
  grandchild of the shape root

  The bug: GetStarCountForShape() used foreach (Transform child in 
  shape.transform) which only iterates over direct children. For multi-tile
  shapes, the star GameObjects (named "Star" / "Star2") are nested one level
   deeper — they're grandchild transforms. So the method always returned 0,
  BoardShapeData.starsAwarded was always 0, and
  CreateShapeFromBoardShapeData never called AddStarVisualToShape() during
  restoration.

  The fix: Changed to shape.GetComponentsInChildren<Transform>(true) which
  searches all descendants recursively, with a guard to skip the root shape
  transform. This catches stars at any nesting level.
